More than swiping.

SXISSOR is designed to help users connect, but also learn, ask questions and feel supported through a community space.

  • Ask dating and profile questions
  • Vote posts up or down
  • Read and write comments
  • Keep conversations respectful

Simple profile control.

Users can update their profile, connect phone and email, submit verification, adjust filters and manage account settings.

  • Edit profile anytime
  • Add account verification
  • Change language and appearance
  • Manage blocked users
