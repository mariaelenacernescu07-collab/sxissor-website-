The flow

Designed to make interest clearer.

SXISSOR keeps the experience simple: discover, wave, match, and talk. The goal is to make dating feel less random and more intentional.

01

Discover real profiles

Browse people nearby with profile photos, age, dating intention, reply style and basic profile details.

02

Send a wave

A wave is a low-pressure way to show interest before starting a full conversation.

03

Match when interest is mutual

When both users wave at each other, SXISSOR opens the match flow and starts the chat.

Settings that matter

Filters, blocked users, verification, account information and safety tools sit in one simple place.

Safety should feel built in, not added later.

SXISSOR is being shaped around user control. People should be able to block, report, verify, and manage their own experience without confusion.

  • Block users and remove them from discovery.
  • Report unsafe, fake or inappropriate behaviour.
  • Review blocked users from settings.
  • Use verification to support trust.

Location without exposing exact location.

The nearby map is designed as a visual discovery tool. The intention is to keep location approximate, not expose someone’s exact position.

  • Nearby discovery without exact pins.
  • Distance-based browsing.
  • Filters for better control.
  • Privacy-first map direction.
