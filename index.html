Safety and privacy

Nearby, never exposed.

Safety must work behind every screen, not only appear as a button.

Control across the app.

Blocking, reporting and account protections are designed to apply throughout the entire experience.

  • Blocking applies to profiles, waves, chats and notifications.
  • Reporting and moderation tools support accountability.
  • Private account data uses server-enforced permissions.
  • Rate limits help reduce spam and abuse.

Location without exposure.

SXISSOR helps people discover who is nearby without revealing individual addresses or live positions.

  • No exact addresses, buildings or live-location pins.
  • Approximate distance and broad privacy zones only.
  • Members control whether location features are enabled.
  • Blocked users cannot use location features to reconnect.
