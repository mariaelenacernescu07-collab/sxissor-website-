Safety controls

Protection across the app.

Safety checks must work behind the buttons, not only appear on screen.

  • Blocking applies to discovery, waves, messages and notifications.
  • Reporting and moderation tools support accountability.
  • Private data access is protected by server-enforced permissions.
  • Rate limits help prevent spam and abuse.
Location privacy

Nearby, never exposed.

SXISSOR helps people connect without publishing exact locations.

  • No exact addresses or live-location pins.
  • No buildings or individual streets displayed.
  • Approximate distance and city-level zones only.
  • Users control whether location features are enabled.
