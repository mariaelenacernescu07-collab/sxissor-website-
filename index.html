The product

A complete connection journey.

Every major feature supports one goal: helping the right people discover, trust and speak to each other.

01 · DISCOVER

Nearby without exposure

Broad privacy zones and approximate distance support discovery without publishing live positions.

02 · SIGNAL

Waves make interest clear

A low-pressure expression of interest. Mutual waves create a private match.

03 · CONVERSE

Real-time messaging

Messages, images, voice notes, unread counts and delivery states keep conversations clear.

04 · MOMENTUM

The three-day match rule

Active conversations stay alive while silent matches make space for new opportunities.

05 · TRUST

Profiles with context

Intentions, reply style, connection count and verification status help people choose thoughtfully.

06 · COMMUNITY

Connection beyond dating

Members can publish posts, reply and participate in a wider community.

Why SXISSOR

Focused by design.

SXISSOR is not trying to be a general social network. Its product decisions are made around one clearly defined community and its needs.

Clear intentions before conversation

Members understand what another person wants before starting a match.

Momentum instead of match collecting

The three-day rule is designed to encourage participation rather than passive swiping.

Community alongside dating

Members can connect even when they are not actively looking for a match.

Privacy as product design

Location and blocking controls shape the core experience rather than appearing as an afterthought.

Trust architecture

Safety behind every screen.

A trustworthy dating product needs protections in the interface, database and operating process.

Member control

People need clear, immediate control over who can find and contact them.

  • Blocking applies across discovery, waves, chat and notifications
  • Reporting and moderation workflows
  • Verification status and trust signals
  • Location features can be disabled

Technical protection

Important rules are enforced behind the mobile interface.

  • Server-enforced database permissions
  • Rate limits to reduce spam and abuse
  • Private media and account controls
  • Approximate location rather than exact pins
