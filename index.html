Built for modern dating

SXISSOR brings together profiles, matching, chat, safety tools and community features in one clean app experience.

Waves & matches

Send a wave, match with people who like you back, and start a conversation when the interest is mutual.

📍

Nearby map

Browse nearby profiles with a map-style experience designed to make local discovery feel simple and visual.

✅

Verification

Account verification helps build trust and supports a safer experience for people using the app.

💬

Clean chat

Messaging includes simple chat, voice-note concepts, and future call or video features for matched users.

🌍

Community

Ask dating questions, vote on posts, read comments and share advice with other users.

⭐

Premium options

Premium features may include who viewed you, profile visibility, unlimited wave history and future AI tips.

More than just matching.

SXISSOR includes a community space where users can ask questions, read advice and vote on helpful answers.

  • Ask dating and profile questions
  • Vote posts up or down
  • Read and write comments
  • Keep conversations respectful

Designed to feel simple.

The app is being built with a clean, dark interface, easy navigation, profile cards, settings, filters and account controls.

  • Simple onboarding
  • Profile completion checklist
  • Language and appearance settings
  • Report and block tools

Safety matters

SXISSOR is being designed with safety features such as verification, reporting, blocking, safety tips and account security options.

🛡️

Report users

Users can report unsafe, fake or inappropriate behaviour directly from profiles.

🚫

Block users

Blocking tools help users control who can contact or appear to them.

🔐

Account security

Phone, email and verification status help make accounts more secure and trustworthy.
